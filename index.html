Step 3

Step 4

Now, go to the body tab, press edit as html, and build your own website!

Tips

Here are some tips to help you build your own website:

  • Use <h1> to <h6> for headings.
  • Use <p> for paragraphs.
  • Use <a href="URL">Link Text</a> for links.
  • Use <img src="image.jpg" alt="description"> for images.
  • Use <ul> and <ol> for lists.
